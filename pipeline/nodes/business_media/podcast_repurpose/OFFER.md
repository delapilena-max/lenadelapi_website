# Offer — podcast_repurpose

## The pitch (one line)

> "I turn your existing videos, podcasts, calls, and business knowledge into a month of
> short-form social content."

## Who it's for

Owner-led businesses that already have raw media sitting unused and no repurposing
system: podcast hosts, coaches/consultants/course creators, local/regional service
businesses with raw iPhone clips and testimonials, any small business posting long-form
with no short-form presence. Full detail:
`pipeline/change_notes/business_media_node_pivot_plan.md` §4.

## Proposed monthly package (hypothesis, not priced/committed yet)

- 8–12 short-form clip ideas per month, each timestamped into the source media.
- Hook line, caption, title/on-screen text, and thumbnail/cover-text suggestion per clip.
- One posting calendar for the month.
- 2–3 reusable CTA variants.
- One bundled content packet.
- One approval packet (explicit sign-off before anything counts as delivered).
- Light analytics/iteration notes after the batch posts.

**This is a starting hypothesis to pressure-test with real prospects — not a locked
SKU or price.** See the pivot memo §5 for the full deliverable list and its caveats
(no edited video, no posting automation, in this MVP).

## Pricing hypothesis (2026-07-07 — unvalidated, do not quote as final)

**Anchor price to test: $997/month** for the single starter package above (8–12 clip
angles, one content packet, one approval packet, one posting calendar, 2–3 CTA
variants, light post-batch notes). Reasoning, not evidence:

- Roughly in line with what solo social-media ghostwriters/repurposing freelancers
  charge for comparable monthly output (a same-order-of-magnitude anchor, not a
  researched market study — no competitor pricing was pulled for this).
- Priced as a plan/strategy deliverable (no edited video), so below what a full
  edited-clips service would charge — the scope boundary in `OFFER.md`'s "what's
  explicitly out of scope" section justifies the lower anchor.
- Deliberately round and simple for a first conversation, not a tiered menu — a
  single number is easier to react to than a pricing table when the real goal of the
  first few conversations is learning what prospects would pay, not closing a deal.

**Pilot ladder (matches pivot memo §12):**
1. First 1–3 prospects: one **free or steeply discounted** pilot batch, in exchange
   for a testimonial and permission to use the result as a portfolio sample.
2. After the pilot batch is seen, ask what they'd actually pay **before** stating the
   $997 anchor — real signal first, anchor second.
3. If their number and the anchor are close, convert to the first paid month at (or
   near) $997/month. If their number is far off in either direction, that's the
   signal to revise this hypothesis, not to hold the anchor rigidly.

**This number has zero real-world validation** — no prospect has seen it, no pilot
has run. Treat it as the opening assumption for the first 1–3 pricing conversations
only, per the pivot memo's Day 4 pilot-outreach step (§10) and Day 7 review-and-adjust
step.

## What's explicitly out of scope for now

- No video editing/cut footage — the deliverable is the plan, not the footage.
- No posting automation (Blotato or otherwise).
- No pricing commitment — validate via the first pilot conversations (see the pivot
  memo §12, "how to sell the first 3 clients manually").

## How the first 3 clients get sold

No landing page, no ads, no funnel. Warm outreach to businesses with a visible gap
(long-form content, no short-form presence), a free/discounted pilot batch in exchange
for a testimonial, then convert to paid once they've seen a real batch and named a
price they'd pay. Full detail: pivot memo §12.

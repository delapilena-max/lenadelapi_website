# Outbound Pitch Script — podcast_repurpose

**Primary framing:** Autonomous Content Operations Engine

**Status:** draft, unsent, untested. Written 2026-07-07 as Day 2 of the pivot memo's
7-day build plan (`pipeline/change_notes/business_media_node_pivot_plan.md` §10). The
wording below has not been shown to a real prospect yet and should stay aligned with
the current $1,250/month managed-pilot offer.

## Who this is for

Warm or lukewarm contacts only, per the pivot memo §12 — people in Nicolas's existing
network (or one intro away), not cold strangers. Target: an owner-led business that
visibly already publishes long-form content (podcast, YouTube, webinar replays, calls,
testimonials, or other expert media) but has little or no short-form/social presence.
That gap should be checkable before sending anything — look at their existing
channels first.

## Core message (DM or email, ~70 words)

> Hey [Name] — been following [Podcast/Channel Name], really liked the [specific
> episode/topic]. Noticed you've got [X episodes / a solid back catalog] and not much
> of it showing up as short clips on Instagram/TikTok.
>
> I run a managed content operations engine for businesses with good long-form
> material already on hand. I take existing podcasts, videos, webinars, calls, or
> testimonials and turn them into a human-reviewed month of short-form content —
> clip picks, hooks, captions, titles, a posting calendar, and an approval-ready
> packet. If useful, I can map a first batch off your last episode or recording.

## Shorter version (cold-ish DM, ~35 words)

> Hey [Name] — noticed [X episodes] and almost no short-form presence yet. I run a
> managed content operations engine that turns existing long-form media into a month
> of reviewed clips, captions, and a posting calendar. Want me to sketch a first
> batch off one episode or recording?

## Slightly longer warm email

> Subject: turning your existing long-form content into a managed clip pipeline
>
> Hey [Name] —
>
> I noticed you already have real material sitting there: [podcast / webinar /
> YouTube / calls / testimonials]. There’s a clear opportunity to turn that into a
> steady short-form content flow without asking you to start from scratch.
>
> I run a managed content operations engine: strategy, repurposing, QA, repair,
> client approval, publishing support, provenance, metrics, and learning. The
> deliverable is a human-reviewed content packet built from what you already have —
> not generic AI posts, not self-serve software, and not a promise of automated
> publishing.
>
> If it’s useful, I can put together a first batch from one episode or recording and
> show you what the monthly workflow would look like.
>
> If that sounds worth a look, I’d suggest a short discovery call or intake review to
> confirm scope and fit.

## If they ask "what's this cost?" before seeing a sample

> The current pilot-ready offer is **$1,250/month** for a managed content operations
> pilot. I’d rather first confirm that the fit is real and the content is there before
> we treat it like a finalized monthly engagement.
>
> The package is a managed, human-approved workflow built from your existing long-form
> media. It is not software, not SaaS, and not a promise of fully automated
> publishing.

## If the pilot batch lands well and they ask what ongoing costs

> That batch was [X] clip ideas off one episode. Ongoing, monthly, doing this off
> whatever you publish, is what I’d run as a managed service. If the batch feels
> useful, the current pilot target is **$1,250/month**.
>
> If you want to move forward, the next step is a short discovery call or intake
> review so I can confirm what raw material you already have and whether the pilot is
> a fit.

## If they ask "what exactly do I get?"

> You get a managed content operation built around your existing long-form material:
> short-form clip picks, hooks, captions, titles, a posting calendar, an approval-ready
> packet, and human review before anything is treated as delivered.
>
> The workflow is strategy -> repurposing -> QA -> repair -> client approval ->
> publishing support -> provenance -> metrics -> learning.

## If they ask about price

> The current managed-pilot price is **$1,250/month**.
>
> That’s for a done-for-you, human-approved content operation, not software and not a
> self-serve system. The scope stays tied to the content you already have.

## If they are interested and need the next step

> Great — the next step is a short discovery call or intake review. I’ll confirm the
> raw media you already have, the content gap, and whether the pilot scope makes sense
> before anything moves forward.

## What NOT to do with this script

- Do not mass-send it — this is a warm/checkable-gap outreach script, not a cold blast
  template (pivot memo §12, point 1).
- Do not describe this as SaaS, self-serve software, or a multi-tenant platform.
- Do not promise edited video, autonomous publishing, or unsupported automation — the
  scope boundary in `OFFER.md`'s "what's explicitly out of scope" section applies to
  this script too.
- Do not send this to anyone without a specific, checkable "long-form content, thin or
  no short-form presence" gap already observed on their own channels.

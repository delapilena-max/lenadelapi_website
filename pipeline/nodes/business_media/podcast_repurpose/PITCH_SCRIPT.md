# Outbound Pitch Script — podcast_repurpose

**Status:** draft, unsent, untested. Written 2026-07-07 as Day 2 of the pivot memo's
7-day build plan (`pipeline/change_notes/business_media_node_pivot_plan.md` §10). This
wording has not been shown to a real prospect yet — expect to revise it after the
first few real conversations (§12's outreach step).

## Who this is for

Warm or lukewarm contacts only, per the pivot memo §12 — people in Nicolas's existing
network (or one intro away), not cold strangers. Target: an owner-led business that
visibly already publishes long-form content (podcast, YouTube, webinar replays) but has
little or no short-form/social presence. That gap should be checkable before sending
anything — look at their existing channels first.

## Core message (DM or email, ~70 words)

> Hey [Name] — been following [Podcast/Channel Name], really liked the [specific
> episode/topic]. Noticed you've got [X episodes / a solid back catalog] and not much
> of it showing up as short clips on Instagram/TikTok.
>
> I turn existing podcasts/videos/calls into a month of short-form content — clip
> picks, hooks, captions, titles, a posting calendar, ready for you or your editor to
> use. Want me to run one free batch off your last episode so you can see it before
> anything's a paid thing?

## Shorter version (cold-ish DM, ~35 words)

> Hey [Name] — noticed [X episodes] and no short-form presence yet. I turn existing
> podcast/video content into a month of ready-to-post clips, captions, and a posting
> calendar. Want a free sample batch off one episode?

## If they ask "what's this cost?" before seeing a sample

> Let's not worry about that yet — I'd rather you see a real batch off your own
> content first, free, then we can talk price based on whether it's actually useful to
> you. [Anchor price is not stated here — see `OFFER.md`'s pricing hypothesis; the
> pivot memo §12 explicitly wants their number before ours.]

## If the pilot batch lands well and they ask what ongoing costs

> That batch was [X] clip ideas off one episode. Ongoing, monthly, doing this off
> whatever you publish, is what I'd charge for after — before I throw out a number,
> what would you expect to pay for something like this monthly?
>
> (Only after they answer: reference `OFFER.md`'s $997/month anchor as your own
> starting point, not before.)

## What NOT to do with this script

- Do not mass-send it — this is a warm/checkable-gap outreach script, not a cold blast
  template (pivot memo §12, point 1).
- Do not quote the $997/month anchor unprompted — get their number first (pivot memo
  §12, point 4; `OFFER.md`'s pricing hypothesis).
- Do not promise edited video or posting automation — the scope boundary in
  `OFFER.md`'s "what's explicitly out of scope" section applies to this script too.
- Do not send this to anyone without a specific, checkable "long-form content, thin or
  no short-form presence" gap already observed on their own channels.

How to request deletion of your lenadelapi_website data

Effective date: 2026-06-01

What we delete

When you request deletion we will remove all personal data we store about you related to lenadelapi_website, including:

  • OAuth tokens and app‑scoped IDs
  • Profile information synced from social accounts
  • Posts, drafts, and media uploaded through the app
  • Backups and logs associated with your account (subject to lawful retention exceptions)

How to request deletion

Option A — Email (recommended): Send an email from the address associated with your lenadelapi_website account to delapilena@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my lenadelapi_website data. Include your lenadelapi_website username or the email used to sign up.

Option B — Automated (if available): If you previously authorized lenadelapi_website and we have a stored token, you may POST a JSON request to our automated endpoint:

POST https://delapilena-max.github.io/lenadelapi_website/data-deletion-github
Content-Type: application/json

{ "userId": "YOUR_INTERNAL_USER_ID" }

If the automated endpoint is not available for your account, use the email method above.

What happens next

  1. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and provide a confirmation code.
  2. We will begin deletion and update the status at a public status URL we provide with the confirmation code.
  3. When deletion completes we will email you a final confirmation.

Retention exceptions

We may retain minimal records for legal compliance or fraud prevention as permitted by law. If that applies we will notify you and explain what is retained and why.

Contact

If you have questions, email delapilena@gmail.com.
